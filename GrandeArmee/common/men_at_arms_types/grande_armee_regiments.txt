# Recruited and paid for like any other men-at-arms, not conjured. They sit
# above the best the medieval world can field -- gendarmes and cataphracts top
# out around 125 damage, and the median unit in the game is 30 -- without
# reaching the absurd. A large enough coalition can still grind them down.
